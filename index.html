Rooted in tradition. Built for how you actually learn today.

IA Hidayah Hub Academy grew out of a simple belief: that learning the Qur'an should be personal, unhurried, and rigorous — not rushed through a group call with twenty other students.

Every class is one-on-one, tailored to the student's pace, whether that's a child memorizing their first surah or an adult correcting years-old Tajweed habits. Classes run over WhatsApp, Zoom, or Google Meet — whatever works for your connection and schedule.
