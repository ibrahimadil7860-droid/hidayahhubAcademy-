Bacchon Ko Tajweed Sikhane Ka Tareeqa

IA Hidayah Hub Academy · Parents Ke Liye Rehnuma

Bacchon ko Tajweed sikhana kabhi kabhi mushkil lagta hai, khaas kar jab wo jaldi bore ho jayein ya interest kho dein. Lekin sahi tareeqe aur thodi sabar ke sath, ye process kaafi asaan aur khushgawar ban sakta hai.

Chhote Sessions, Zyada Asar

Bacchon ka attention span bado se kam hota hai. 15-20 minute ki chhoti, focused classes lambe sessions se zyada mufeed sabit hoti hain. Isi liye one-on-one online classes mein teacher bacche ki pace ke mutabiq dhal sakta hai.

Makharij Ko Khel Ki Tarah Sikhayein

Har harf ki sahi adaigi (makhraj) sikhane ke liye teachers aksar mimicry aur repetition ka tareeqa istemal karte hain — bacche ke liye ye ek game jaisa lagta hai, na ke rote-learning. Isse retention behtar hoti hai aur bacche khush rehte hain.

Ghar Par Practice Kaise Karwayein

Class ke bahar bhi thodi practice zaroori hai. Roz sirf 5-10 minute, jo class mein seekha usay dohrana kaafi hota hai. Walidain ka is mein shamil hona — chahe khud Tajweed na jaante hon, sirf sun kar encourage karna — bacche ki motivation barhata hai.

Sabr Aur Consistency

Har bacche ki seekhne ki raftar mukhtalif hoti hai. Kisi ko kisi makhraj mein zyada waqt lagta hai, aur ye bilkul normal hai. Jaldi mein zabardasti karne se bacche ka interest khatam ho sakta hai — sabr sabse behtareen hathiyar hai.
