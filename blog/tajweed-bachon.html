PARENTING & TAJWEED

Bacchon Ko Tajweed Sikhane Ka Tareeqa

Bacchon ko Tajweed sikhana kabhi kabhi mushkil lagta hai, khaas kar jab wo jaldi bore ho jayein. Lekin sahi tareeqe aur thodi sabar ke sath, ye process kaafi asaan aur khushgawar ban sakta hai.

Har walid aur walida chahte hain ke unka bacha Qur'an ko sahi tarah, Tajweed ke sath padhe. Lekin bacchon ka dimagh bado se mukhtalif tarah kaam karta hai — unka attention span chhota hota hai, aur wo rote-learning se jaldi ub jate hain. Is guide mein hum practical, aazmaaye hue tareeqe share kar rahe hain jo humari academy mein roz istemal hote hain.

Chhote Sessions, Zyada Asar

Bacchon ka attention span bado se kam hota hai — aam taur par 5 se 10 saal ki umar ke bacchon ke liye 15-20 minute ki focused class, 45 minute ki lambi class se kahin zyada mufeed sabit hoti hai. Isi liye one-on-one online classes mein teacher bacche ki pace ke mutabiq dhal sakta hai, na ke ek fixed schedule ke peeche bhaagta hai.

Agar aap ghar par practice karwa rahe hain, to bhi yehi usool apply hota hai — ek baar mein 5-10 minute kaafi hain, lambay sessions se bacha thak kar interest khona shuru kar deta hai.

Makharij Ko Khel Ki Tarah Sikhayein

Har harf ki sahi adaigi (makhraj) sikhane ke liye teachers aksar mimicry aur repetition ka tareeqa istemal karte hain — bacche ke liye ye ek game jaisa lagta hai, na ke rote-learning. Kuch tareeqe jo kaamyab sabit hote hain:

  • Awaaz ki nakal: Teacher pehle harf bolta hai, bacha nakal karta hai — jaise ek "echo game"
  • Haath ke ishare: Kuch teachers makhraj (jaise halaq, zabaan, honth) ki taraf ishara karte hain taake bacha visually samajh sake
  • Chhoti kahaniyan: Mushkil huroof ko yaad rakhne ke liye chhoti kahaniyan ya tukbandi banana

Isse retention behtar hoti hai aur bacche khush rehte hain — wo seekhte hue mehsoos nahi karte ke unhe "parhai" karwayi ja rahi hai.

Ghar Par Practice Kaise Karwayein

Class ke bahar bhi thodi practice zaroori hai, lekin isay bojh na banayein. Roz sirf 5-10 minute, jo class mein seekha usay dohrana kaafi hota hai. Walidain ka is mein shamil hona — chahe khud Tajweed na jaante hon, sirf sun kar encourage karna — bacche ki motivation barhata hai.

Kuch practical tips:

  • Ek fixed waqt muqarrar karein — jaise Maghrib ke baad — taake ye routine ban jaye
  • Bacche ko khud se nahi, balke sath baith kar sunein, taake wo mehsoos kare ke ye important hai
  • Chhoti kamyabiyon par tareef karein — jaise ek mushkil harf ko sahi adaa karna
  • Galti par foran na toke — pehle poora jumla ya aayat mukammal hone dein, phir nishan-dehi karein

Sabr Aur Consistency

Har bacche ki seekhne ki raftar mukhtalif hoti hai. Kisi ko kisi makhraj mein zyada waqt lagta hai, aur ye bilkul normal hai. Jaldi mein zabardasti karne se bacche ka interest khatam ho sakta hai — sabr sabse behtareen hathiyar hai.

Ye yaad rakhna zaroori hai ke maqsad sirf "jaldi khatam karna" nahi, balke ek mazboot buniyad banana hai jo ziadgi umar tak saath chale. Ek bacha jo sabar se, mazboot buniyad ke sath seekhta hai, bade hoker zyada confident reciter banta hai, chahe shuru mein thora dheema lage.

Kab Fikr Karni Chahiye?

Agar bacha mahinon tak koi pragati nahi dikha raha, ya class mein mukammal tor par disinterested rehta hai, to teacher se khulkar baat karein. Kabhi kabhi teacher ka tareeqa bacche ke mizaj se match nahi karta — aisi surat mein teacher badalna bhi ek sahi faisla ho sakta hai, kisi sharmindagi ki baat nahi.
