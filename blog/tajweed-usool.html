Tajweed Basics

Tajweed Ke 5 Bunyadi Usool

IA Hidayah Hub Academy · Beginner's Guide

Tajweed ka matlab hai Qur'an ko uske sahi tareeqe se, har harf ko uske asal makhraj aur sift ke sath parhna. Yahan panch bunyadi usool hain jo har naye talib-e-ilm ko pehle samajhne chahiye.

1. Makharij (Huroof Ki Sahi Adaigi)

Har Arabic harf mooh, halaq (gale), ya naak ke kisi khaas hisse se nikalta hai. Sahi makhraj seekhna Tajweed ki bunyad hai — is ke bagair maani badal sakti hai.

2. Ghunnah (Naak Se Awaz)

Jab "noon" ya "meem" ke sath tashdeed ho, to awaz naak se guzarti hai — isay ghunnah kehte hain. Ye 2 counts (harkat) tak barqarar rakhi jaati hai.

3. Madd (Awaz Ko Lamba Karna)

Madd un jagahon par hota hai jahan harf ko mutayyan waqt tak lamba karna zaroori hota hai — jaise alif, waw, ya ke sath. Iski lambai (2, 4, ya 6 counts) qaida ke mutabiq badalti hai.

4. Qalqalah (Uchhalti Hui Awaz)

Paanch huroof (ق ط ب ج د) jab sakin (bina harkat) hon, to unki awaz mein ek halki si "uchhal" aati hai — isay qalqalah kehte hain.

5. Waqf (Rukne Ke Usool)

Qur'an mein khaas nishaniyan hoti hain jo batati hain kahan rukna chahiye, kahan nahi, aur kahan ikhtiyari hai. Sahi waqf se aayat ka maani sahi tarah adaa hota hai.
