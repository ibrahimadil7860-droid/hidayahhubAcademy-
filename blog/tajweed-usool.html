TAJWEED BASICS

Tajweed Ke 5 Bunyadi Usool

Tajweed ka matlab hai Qur'an ko uske sahi tareeqe se, har harf ko uske asal makhraj aur sift ke sath parhna. Yahan panch bunyadi usool hain jo har naye talib-e-ilm ko pehle samajhne chahiye.

Tajweed seekhna shuru mein mushkil lag sakta hai — itni saari istilahen (terms), itne saare qawaid. Lekin agar inhe panch bunyadi categories mein taqseem kar diya jaye, to poori tasveer bohot saaf ho jati hai. Agar aap Tajweed ke tafseeli qawaid parhna chahte hain, to humara mukammal rehnuma bhi zaroor dekhein — yahan hum sirf bunyadi taaruf de rahe hain.

1. Makharij (Huroof Ki Sahi Adaigi)

Har Arabic harf mooh, halaq (gale), ya naak ke kisi khaas hisse se nikalta hai. Sahi makhraj seekhna Tajweed ki bunyad hai — is ke bagair maani badal sakti hai. Misal ke taur par, "ha" (ح) aur "kha" (خ) ki awazein bohot qareeb lagti hain lekin unka makhraj mukhtalif hai, aur ghalat adaa karne se lafz ka maani bhi badal sakta hai.

Makharij ko paanch bare groups mein taqseem kiya jata hai: khaali halaq-o-mun (madd huroof), halaq, zabaan (sabse zyada huroof isi se nikalte hain), hont, aur naak (ghunnah ke liye). Har group ke apne khaas huroof hain, aur inhe pehchaan na pehla qadam hai.

2. Ghunnah (Naak Se Awaz)

Jab "noon" ya "meem" ke sath tashdeed ho, to awaz naak se guzarti hai — isay ghunnah kehte hain. Ye 2 counts (harkat) tak barqarar rakhi jaati hai. Ghunnah sirf tashdeed wale noon/meem tak mehdood nahi — ye Noon Sakin aur Tanween ke kuch ahkaam (jaise Idgham aur Ikhfa) mein bhi aati hai.

Beginners ke liye ghunnah ko sahi tarah mehsoos karna shuru mein mushkil hota hai — is liye teacher ke saamne baar baar practice karna zaroori hai, taake aap apni awaz mein farq mehsoos kar sakein.

3. Madd (Awaz Ko Lamba Karna)

Madd un jagahon par hota hai jahan harf ko mutayyan waqt tak lamba karna zaroori hota hai — jaise alif, waw, ya ke sath. Iski lambai (2, 4, ya 6 counts) qaida ke mutabiq badalti hai.

Sabse buniyadi madd — Madd Tabee'i — hamesha 2 harkat ki hoti hai, aur ye har madd huroof ke sath aati hai jab koi hamza ya sukoon na ho. Jab hamza ya sukoon shamil ho jaye, to madd ki lambai barh sakti hai — ye "Madd Far'ee" kehlata hai, jiski kai qismein hain.

4. Qalqalah (Uchhalti Hui Awaz)

Paanch huroof (ق ط ب ج د) jab sakin (bina harkat) hon, to unki awaz mein ek halki si "uchhal" aati hai — isay qalqalah kehte hain. Ye huroof yaad rakhne ke liye "قطب جد" (Qutb Jad) lafz istemal hota hai.

Qalqalah do tarah ki hoti hai — chhoti (sughra), jab ye huroof lafz ke beech mein sakin hon, aur badi (kubra), jab waqf (rukne) ki wajah se ye huroof lafz ke aakhir mein sakin ho jayein. Kubra ki awaz sughra se thori zyada numayan hoti hai.

5. Waqf (Rukne Ke Usool)

Qur'an mein khaas nishaniyan hoti hain jo batati hain kahan rukna chahiye, kahan nahi, aur kahan ikhtiyari hai. Sahi waqf se aayat ka maani sahi tarah adaa hota hai — ghalat jagah rukne se maani badal sakta hai, is liye ye usool bhi utna hi zaroori hai jitna makharij ya madd.

Mushaf mein ye alamatein chhote Arabic huroof ki soorat mein likhi hoti hain — jaise "مـ" (waqf laazim, rukna zaroori) ya "لا" (na ruko). In alamaton ko pehchanna aur samajhna ek tajربaکار (experienced) qari banne ki taraf ek ahem qadam hai.

وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا

"Aur Qur'an ko thehar thehar kar, saaf saaf parho." — Surah Al-Muzzammil, 73:4

Practice Se Hi Mahaarat Aati Hai

Ye usool parh kar samajhna asaan hai, lekin inhe amal mein lana ek qualified teacher ki rehnumai ke bagair mushkil hota hai. Real-time correction hi asal farq dalta hai — kitabon ya videos se parh kar aap nazariyati (theoretical) samajh to hasil kar sakte hain, lekin apni khud ki ghalatiyan sunna aur pehchanna mushkil hota hai bina kisi doosre ke saamne tilawat kiye.
