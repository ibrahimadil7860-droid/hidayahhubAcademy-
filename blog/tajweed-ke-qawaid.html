TAJWEED GUIDE

Tajweed Ke Tamam Qawaid — Mukammal Rehnuma

Tajweed Quran ko uske sahi makhraj aur sifaat ke sath, ghalti se bacha kar padhne ka ilm hai. Neeche Tajweed ke tamam bunyadi qawaid tarteeb se, aasan misalon ke sath diye gaye hain.

Note: Ye guide sirf reading ke liye hai. Sahih talaffuz (pronunciation) sirf qualified ustaad se seedha sun kar aur practice karke aata hai — humari free trial class book karein.

1. Tajweed Kya Hai?

Lughat mein "Tajweed" ka matlab hai "acha karna" ya "behtar banana". Istilah mein iska matlab hai Quran ki har aayat ko uske huroof ke sahi makhraj (nikalne ki jaga) aur sifaat (khususiyaat) ke sath, bila kami beshi ke padhna. Ulama ke nazdeek Tajweed ke sath Quran padhna "fard-e-kifayah" hai — yani agar kuch log is ilm ko seekh lein aur sahih tilawat karein, to baqi ummat bari ho jati hai, lekin har musalman ke liye apni tilawat ko galtiyon se bachana zaroori hai.

2. Makharij-e-Huroof (Huroof Kahan Se Nikalte Hain)

Har Arabi harf mun ke kisi khaas hisse se nikalta hai. Makharij ko 5 bade groups mein taqseem kiya jata hai:

Makhraj
  • Al-Jawf (Khaali Halaq aur Mun): madd huroof — Alif, Waw (sakin), Ya (sakin)
  • Al-Halq (Halaq): Hamza, Ha, Ain, Ha, Ghain, Kha
  • Al-Lisan (Zubaan): zyada tar huroof — Qaf, Kaf, Jeem, Sheen, Ya, Dhad, Lam, Noon, Ra, Ta, Dal, Ta, Za, Tha, Zal, Seen, Saad, Zaa
  • Ash-Shafatain (Dono Hont): Fa, Ba, Meem, Waw (mutaharrik)
  • Al-Khaishoom (Naak): Ghunnah — jab bhi Meem ya Noon mushaddad ho, ya Ikhfa/Idgham/Iqlab mein ghunnah aaye

3. Sifaat-e-Huroof (Huroof Ki Khususiyaat)

Har harf ki apni mustaqil sifaat hoti hain jo uski awaaz ko doosre huroof se mumtaz karti hain. Kuch ahem jorray:

  • Hams / Jahr: Hams mein sans nikalti hai (jaise Fa, Ha, Sa), Jahr mein sans rukti hai (baqi huroof)
  • Shiddah / Rakhawah / Tawassut: awaaz rukne, behne, ya darmiyani halat mein nikalne se mutalliq
  • Isti'la / Istifal: Isti'la mein zaban oopar (mota/bhari sound) — Khaas 7 huroof: Kh, Sad, Dhad, Ghain, Ta, Qaf, Za (yaad rakhne ka lafz: "Khus Sadh Ghatt Qiz")
  • Qalqalah: aage tafseel mein

4. Noon Sakin Aur Tanween Ke Ahkaam

Jab Noon Sakin (ن ساکن) ya Tanween (ً ٍ ٌ) ke baad koi harf aaye, to 4 mumkin ahkaam hote hain:

Izhar (اظہار)

Halqi Izhar

Agar Noon Sakin/Tanween ke baad halaq ke 6 huroof aayein — Hamza, Ha, Ain, Ha, Ghain, Kha — to Noon ko poora saaf (clear) padha jata hai, bila ghunnah ke izafi.

Idgham (ادغام)

Idgham Ma'al Ghunnah aur Bila Ghunnah

Agar baad mein Ya, Noon, Meem, Waw (یَنْمُو) aayein, to Noon Sakin/Tanween unme mila diya jata hai, ghunnah ke sath (2 harkat).

Agar baad mein Lam ya Ra aayein, to bila ghunnah ke idgham hota hai.

Iqlab (اقلاب)

Sirf Ek Harf Ke Sath

Agar baad mein sirf Ba (ب) aaye, to Noon Sakin/Tanween ko Meem ki awaaz mein badal diya jata hai, ghunnah ke sath, honth band kar ke.

Ikhfa (اخفاء)

Baqi 15 Huroof

Baqi mane huay 15 huroof (Ta, Tha, Jeem, Dal, Zal, Za, Seen, Sheen, Sad, Dhad, Ta, Za, Fa, Qaf, Kaf) ke sath Noon Sakin/Tanween ko izhar aur idgham ke darmiyan, naak se ghunnah ke sath chupa kar padha jata hai.

5. Meem Sakin Ke Ahkaam

Meem Sakin (م ساکن) ke 3 ahkaam hain:

  • Ikhfa Shafawi: jab baad mein Ba (ب) aaye — Meem ko honthon se halka chupa kar, ghunnah ke sath padha jata hai
  • Idgham Shafawi (Mutamathilain): jab baad mein doosri Meem aaye — dono ek mein mil jati hain, ghunnah ke sath
  • Izhar Shafawi: baqi tamam huroof ke sath — Meem ko saaf, poora padha jata hai (khaas taur par Fa aur Waw ke sath ehtiyat zaroori hai)

6. Qalqalah

Qalqalah un 5 huroof ki khaas jumping/echoing awaaz hai jab woh sakin hon: Qaf, Ta, Ba, Jeem, Dal — yaad rakhne ka lafz "قطب جد" (Qutb Jad).

  • Qalqalah Sughra (chhoti): jab ye huroof lafz ke darmiyan sakin hon
  • Qalqalah Kubra (bari): jab ye huroof waqf (rukne) ki wajah se sakin ho jayein, lafz ke aakhir mein

7. Ghunnah

Ghunnah naak se aane wali woh khaas awaaz hai jo Meem aur Noon ke sath hoti hai, khaas taur par jab woh mushaddad (شدّہ) hon — is waqt 2 harkat tak khinchi jati hai.

8. Madd Ke Qawaid (Lambai Ke Ahkaam)

Madd ka matlab hai awaaz ko lamba karna. Iski do bari qismein hain:

Madd Asli

Madd Tabee'i

Ye woh buniyadi madd hai jo har madd huroof (Alif, Waw sakin, Ya sakin) ke sath hoti hai, bila kisi hamza ya sukoon ki wajah ke. Lambai: 2 harkat.

Madd Far'ee

Hamza Ya Sukoon Ki Wajah Se Madd

  • Madd Muttasil: madd harf ke baad usi lafz mein hamza aaye — 4-5 harkat, wajib
  • Madd Munfasil: madd harf ke baad agle lafz mein hamza aaye — 4-5 harkat
  • Madd Aarid Lis-Sukoon: waqf ki wajah se aakhri harf sakin ho jaye — 2, 4, ya 6 harkat, qari ki marzi
  • Madd Laazim: madd harf ke baad sabit sukoon (kalimi ya harfi, mukhaffaf ya muthaqqal) — 6 harkat, sabse zyada lambi
  • Madd Badal: hamza pehle, madd harf baad mein (jaise آمَنُوا) — 2 harkat
  • Madd Silah: Ha-e-Zameer (ہ) ke baad agar madd ban jaye — Sughra 2 harkat, Kubra hamza ke sath 4-5 harkat
  • Madd Iwad: waqf par Tanween Fatha (ً) ko Alif ki tarah 2 harkat khencha jata hai

9. Lam Ke Ahkaam (Lafz-e-Jalalah "الله")

Allah ke naam mein Lam ki do halatein hain:

  • Tafkheem (mota/bhari): jab is se pehle Fatha ya Damma ho — jaise "قَالَ اللّٰهُ"
  • Tarqeeq (patla/halka): jab is se pehle Kasra ho — jaise "بِاللّٰهِ"

10. Ra (ر) Ke Ahkaam

  • Tafkheem: jab Ra par Fatha/Damma ho, ya sakin ho aur pehle Fatha/Damma ho
  • Tarqeeq: jab Ra par Kasra ho, ya sakin ho aur pehle Kasra ho (baaz istisnaon ke sath)

11. Waqf Ke Nishanat (Rukne Ki Alamatein)

Mushaf mein chhoti alamatein batati hain keh kahan rukna behtar hai:

  • مـ (Waqf Laazim): rukna zaroori
  • لا: na ruko
  • ج: rukna aur na rukna dono jaiz
  • صلى: na rukna behtar
  • قلى: rukna behtar
  • ∴ ∴ (Muanaqah): in do jagon mein se sirf ek par ruko
