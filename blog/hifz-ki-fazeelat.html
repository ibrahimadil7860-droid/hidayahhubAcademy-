FAZEELAT

Hifz-e-Quran Aur Tilawat Ki Fazeelat

Quran seekhna, yaad karna aur usko sahi tarah se padhna — in sab ki fazeelat khud Rasoolullah (ﷺ) ki zubaan-e-mubarak se sabit hai. Neeche sirf woh ahadees darj hain jo Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim ya Jami at-Tirmidhi mein tasdeeq shuda (authentic) hain, apne hawale ke sath.

1. Quran Seekhna Aur Sikhana

Rasoolullah (ﷺ) ne farmaya: "Tum mein sabse behtar wo hai jo Quran seekhe aur usko (aage) sikhaye."

Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 5027

2. Maahir-e-Quran Farishton Ke Sath

Umm-ul-Mumineen Aisha (رضی اللہ عنہا) riwayat karti hain keh Nabi (ﷺ) ne farmaya: "Jo Quran mein maahir (mahir) hai, wo un buland martabat, farmabardar farishton ke sath hoga jo (a'maal) likhte hain, aur jo Quran padhte waqt atak-atak kar padhe aur usko mushkil lage, uske liye do (guna) ajr hai."

Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 4937 · Sahih Muslim, Hadith 798

Muttafaq Alayh — dono Sihah Sitta ki sab se authentic kitabon mein maujood

3. Quran Padhne Wale Momin Ki Misal

Abu Musa Al-Ash'ari (رضی اللہ عنہ) se riwayat hai keh Rasoolullah (ﷺ) ne farmaya: "Us momin ki misal jo Quran padhta hai, us 'utrujjah' (khushbudar phal) ki tarah hai jiski khushbu bhi achi hai aur zaiqa bhi achha; aur jo momin Quran nahi padhta, uski misal khajoor ki tarah hai — zaiqa mitha, magar khushbu nahi."

Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 5020 / 5427 · Sahih Muslim, Hadith 797

4. Qayamat Ke Din "Recite Aur Chado" Ka Elaan

Abdullah bin Amr (رضی اللہ عنہ) se riwayat hai keh Nabi (ﷺ) ne farmaya: "Sahib-e-Quran (Quran wale) se kaha jayega: Padho aur (Jannat ke darajaat mein) chadte jao, jis tarah duniya mein thehr-thehr kar padhte the; kyunke tumhara martaba wahi hoga jahan tumhari aakhri aayat khatam hogi."

Jami at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 2914 · Sunan Abu Dawood

Imam Tirmidhi ne "Hasan Sahih" qarar diya; Shaykh Al-Albani ne bhi Sahih kaha (Silsilah as-Sahihah 2240)

Chand Faide, In Ahadees Ki Roshni Mein

  • Quran seekhna aur sikhana, dono barabar ke ajr wale amal hain — is liye teaching bhi ek ibadat hai
  • Jo Quran padhte hue mushkil mehsoos karta hai, uska ajr kam nahi hota — balke dugna ho jata hai
  • Hifz aur mustaqil tilawat, dono ka apna maqam hai — jo yaad na kar sake wo bhi barabar ajr ka mustahiq hai bashart ke koshish jari rakhe
  • Jannat mein darajaat ka ta'alluq is baat se hoga keh duniya mein kitna Quran yaad kiya aur padha
Hifz-e-Quran ek safar hai, manzil nahi — har ayat jo yaad hoti hai, wo Akhirat mein ek darja hai.
