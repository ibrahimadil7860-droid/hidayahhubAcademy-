Getting Started

Ghar Baithe Qur'an Kaise Seekhein

IA Hidayah Hub Academy · Guide for New Students

Bohot se log Qur'an seekhna chahte hain lekin waqt, distance, ya sahi ustaad na milne ki wajah se shuruaat nahi kar paate. Alhamdulillah, ab online Qur'an classes ne ye masla kaafi had tak hal kar diya hai — bas ek smartphone aur internet connection chahiye.

Sahi Teacher Ka Intekhab Kaise Karein

Sabse pehla aur sabse ahem qadam hai sahi teacher chunna. Dekh lein ke teacher Hafiz-e-Qur'an hai ya nahi, unki Tajweed ki samajh kitni gehri hai, aur wo aapko naye siray se kis tarah guide karenge. Ek acha teacher sirf lafzi ghalatiyan theek nahi karta, balke sabar se sahi tareeqa sikhata hai.

Pehla Mahina Kaisa Hota Hai

Shuru mein teacher aapki current level assess karta hai — chahe aap bilkul beginner hon ya pehle se thora parhna jaante hon. Pehle hafte mein bunyadi tajweed ke usool aur makharij (huroof ki sahi adaigi) par focus hota hai. Dheere dheere aap poori aayat sahi tarah parhna shuru kar dete hain.

وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

Consistency Sabse Zaroori Hai

Qur'an seekhna ek safar hai, sprint nahi. Haftay mein 2-3 din bhi agar consistent classes ho jayein, to kuch mahinon mein farq saaf nazar aata hai. Jaldi mein zyada wala nahi, balke regular thora seekhna zyada asar-andaz hota hai.

Ghar Baithe Seekhne Ke Fawaid

Online classes ka sabse bara faida flexibility hai — aap apne ghar ke aaram mein, apni pasandeeda waqt par class le sakte hain. Safar ka waqt bachta hai, aur bachon ke liye bhi ye zyada mehfooz aur convenient hota hai.
