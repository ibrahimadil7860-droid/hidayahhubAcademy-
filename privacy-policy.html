2. Kaunsi Maloomat Hum Jama Karte Hain

Hum sirf wo maloomat jama karte hain jo aap khud humein dete hain, ya jo aapki device automatically bhejti hai jab aap website visit karte hain:

  • Aap khud jo dete hain: naam, WhatsApp number, email address, umar/class ka level, aur jo bhi paigham aap enrollment ya inquiry form/WhatsApp/email ke zariye bhejte hain.
  • Payment se mutalliq maloomat: agar aap kisi paid course/digital product ke liye payment karte hain, to transaction ki basic tafseel (jaise amount aur date) rakhi ja sakti hai; card ya bank ki mukammal tafseel humare paas store nahi hoti — ye seedha payment processor (jaise Gumroad ya bank) sambhalta hai.
  • Website usage data: browser type, device, approximate location (country/city level), aur website par kaunse pages dekhe gaye — ye Google Analytics jaise tools ke zariye automatically record ho sakta hai.
  • Cookies: website chhote cookies use kar sakti hai taake aapka tajurba behtar bane aur (agar advertising active ho) relevant ads dikhaye ja sakein.

  • Aapko sahi course, teacher aur class schedule se jorne ke liye

Hum aapki maloomat kabhi bhi bechte nahi, aur na hi kisi teesre fareeq ko marketing maqsad ke liye faraham karte hain.
